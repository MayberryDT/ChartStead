## Primary requirements

1. Custom call-for-speakers forms with conditional logic and category-based routing.
2. A self-service speaker portal for bios, headshots, slides, and supporting documents.
3. Templated speaker email, reminders, and calendar invitations for Gmail, Outlook, and iCal.
4. Submission evaluation and scoring across multiple rounds; AI assistance is optional.
5. Drag-and-drop agenda building with room, track, and speaker conflict detection plus list/day/week/track/room views.
6. A live view of speakers with incomplete onboarding tasks.
7. One-way Accelevents integration to avoid duplicate registration-platform entry.
8. Speaker-portal resources/wiki pages with HTML embeds.
9. Mobile-friendly, embeddable speaker and schedule views.

The brief explicitly says an exact Sessionboard design clone is unnecessary. A good-enough open-source replacement is the goal.

## Competition rules in this version

- Deadline: Wednesday, August 12 at 10 PM Pacific.
- Submission: form, open-source repository, deployed test site, and walkthrough.
- Valid submissions may request up to $500 in token-cost reimbursement with proof.
- The AI Engineer team evaluates submissions independently.
- Product judgment is the tiebreaker: the winner should feel like something the team would actually use or buy.
- Prize: $10,000 and a walkthrough/interview for a Latent Space write-up.
- Any stack is allowed. Small bonuses are mentioned for Cloudflare, Airtable persistence, Forge hosting, speed, and an API.
