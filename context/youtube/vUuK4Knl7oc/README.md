## Main points

- The customer currently pays more than $40,000 per year and wants an open-source replacement focused on the event-program workflow.
- The main lifecycle is: collect speaker submissions, evaluate them, accept or reject them, schedule accepted sessions, communicate with speakers, and track their remaining tasks.
- Program functionality matters much more than Sessionboard's CRM, marketing, CMS, or AI features.
- Exact visual fidelity is not required. The job-to-be-done and sensible product decisions matter more.
- Performance is an opportunity: the walkthrough repeatedly calls out Sessionboard's slowness.
