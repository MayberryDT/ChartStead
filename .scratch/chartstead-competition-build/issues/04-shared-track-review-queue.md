# 04 — Shared track review queue

**What to build:** A conversational small-committee review workspace where reviewers see the shared submission queue for their assigned tracks, open stable proposal permalinks, leave committee notes, and change reversible internal decisions without contacting speakers.

**Blocked by:** 02 — First proposal end to end.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An administrator can grant a reviewer responsibility for one or more tracks.
- [ ] A reviewer sees all submissions in assigned tracks and no submissions outside those tracks.
- [ ] An administrator retains event-wide submission visibility.
- [ ] Every submission row and detail view resolves through its stable permalink.
- [ ] The review workspace follows the locked submissions master-detail visual direction.
- [ ] Reviewers can search, filter by status and track, sort, and preserve their list context while opening proposals.
- [ ] Reviewers can read the full proposal and speaker context and add committee-only notes.
- [ ] Reviewers can mark unreviewed, approve, maybe, or deny and later change that internal decision.
- [ ] Changing a decision records an audit event and never queues or sends speaker email.
- [ ] Speakers and unauthorized reviewers cannot read committee notes or internal decisions.
- [ ] Acceptance tests cover track authorization, stable deep links, reversible decisions, audit history, and absence of implicit email.
